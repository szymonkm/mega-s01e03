Parser problem

The shared parser reads month first. Day-first tenants therefore get swapped dates. This affects app and email reports, plus forms whose hints agree with their tenant’s date order.

For example, report R-41200 from harbour.dental describes an April booking appearing in July. Report R-41205 belongs here despite its hint intake tag: morrow.tax uses a day-first locale and this report came through the app.

Fix the parser’s date contract. Use an explicit date format or structured date fields. Verify ambiguous and unambiguous dates against each tenant’s locale.

Form-hint problem

The booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY for day-first tenants. People follow that hint, but the form interprets their entry in the tenant’s order. This is a separate bug and needs a separate fix.

Reports come through the form for alder.physio, copper.studio, and pale.harbour. Report R-41230 belongs here despite its parser intake tag. Fix the displayed hint and make its format agree with the input interpretation. Test all affected tenant configurations.
